## RD-A01 — authority receipt and transaction boundary
The existing last-meta format 2 remains readable. V405 adds flat receiptVersion:1 fields recording the actual durable LocalStorage rd_last read at the beginning of an IndexedDB mirror write. Volatile safeStorage fallback is not used as durable evidence. When the observed bytes differ and are at most 3,200,000 characters, retain exact observed primary bytes along with length/checksum; larger or unreadable data is not ranked. The receipt is written with last and last-meta in one IndexedDB transaction. Synchronous failures explicitly abort the transaction; completion alone reports success.

Decision table:
- Equal valid local/mirror bytes: aligned; existing local authority.
- Valid divergent documents and exact receipt match to observed primary: evidence of a mirror committed with this primary, not proof of recency (ABA/rollback remain possible).
- Legacy receipt, missing observation, mismatched bytes or unavailable data: unresolved; never choose by time or generation.
- Valid divergence: try to retain the mirror in an immutable archive before review, then require explicit external dual-copy backup acknowledgment. If internal archival fails, still provide the existing external export path and disclose failure. The selected local remains active; archived mirror may be restored using the new recovery UI.
- Absent local: retain previous guarded valid-mirror recovery; invalid local or pending journal remains on existing explicit recovery path.

No automatic authority switch, no new database schema and no claim of atomicity across stores. The remaining canonical authority migration requires a single-store document/related-assets/profile commit envelope, legacy fallback and validated crash/rollback protocol.

## RD-A03 — selected archive recovery
A new “بازیابی از آرشیو” action lists retained LocalStorage/IndexedDB entries. Selection previews name, device/cable/revision counts; malformed data and invalid mirror checksum are rejected. Selected archive can be exported as portable NRD independently of restoration. Historical local types/catalog/draft assets are used when available; missing historical resources and legacy conversion limitations are disclosed; missing or unsafe referenced SVG blocks the operation.

Direct restore uses prepareRoomImport and applyRoomImport rather than writing raw archive keys. Before replacement, export a normal portable NRD of the active drawing plus raw recovery evidence (local keys, mirror metadata and current drafts), require explicit external retention acknowledgment, recheck live identity/epoch, document/catalog/types/drafts/library, durable local bytes, pending state, selected archive and current mirror, and preserve the previous mirror transactionally. The restored drawing gets a new ID; saved profiles are not replaced by raw archive data. Existing import journal owns success/rollback; the restore wrapper does not claim success prematurely.

## Remaining / limitations
- OPEN-CROSS-STORE: no canonical cross-store commit authority; exact receipts are diagnostic and may increase quota use.
- Direct restore applies to the archived active drawing, not all archived profiles/settings/journals; normal Import may update global catalog/types, explicitly disclosed. Archived raw sources are never deleted. Legacy import may transform/remove unsupported items after explicit warning.
- Missing historical assets, unreadable IDB or unstable local state may prevent restore; raw archive export remains separate. Retention/pruning and full disaster recovery remain open.
- Eight authored regression scenarios: two receipt checks, transaction/guard source contract, archive decoder, restore ordering contract, canceled/declined backup, changed mirror/failed archive and confirmed new-ID restore. NOT_RUN; no Test/Build/Browser/Device execution under POLICY. Acceptance must use isolated browser data and real NRD files.
