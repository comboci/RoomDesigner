## Findings and remediation
1. RD407-A03-PRESTAGE-MARKER: V406 staged the IDB restore intent before writing the local marker during startup replay. A crash followed by unavailable IDB could leave a session unaware of the pending intent. V407 writes and verifies `prepare:ID` before staging, replaces it with plain ID before projection, and blocks writes on uncertain staging. A prepare marker with no intent is cleared only after successful IDB inspection showing no matching completion receipt or old journal; plain markers require final proof. Existing plain V406 markers/intents remain readable.
2. RD407-A03-COMPLETED-RECEIPT-PROOF: previously marker cleanup relied on the receipt ID alone. Verify the exact stored history/receipt, chosen before/target values, current canonical checkpoint and mirror/meta before clearing, including rollback. Protect the latest receipt's history from pruning. A third state blocks recovery rather than rewriting it.
3. RD407-A01-LEGACY-JOURNAL-REVIEW: a session with incomplete legacy journal skipped the startup conflict gate; manual acknowledgment could clear the last marker and permit saving before re-review. Export now includes current editor, types/catalog/drafts, stored SVG/shortcuts and exact raw alternatives; compare bytes rather than only checksum; refuse acknowledgment on unavailable or changed mirror; recheck local and editor state after external export and confirmations. After the final acknowledgment, block writes and editing and require reload. Shortcut commands check recovery state before direct writes.
